Which BitAxe Should You Buy?

For most people: BitAxe Gamma. It offers the best efficiency (lowest J/TH), meaning you get the most hashrate per watt. At $179, it hits the sweet spot between price and performance. The Gamma is the most popular model in the community for good reason.

On a tight budget: BitAxe Ultra. At $149, it's the most affordable way to start mining Bitcoin. You sacrifice a bit of hashrate compared to the Gamma, but the difference is small. Perfect for first-time miners.

Want maximum hashrate: BitAxe Max. With dual BM1397 chips, the Max delivers 2–3 TH/s in a single board. It costs more and uses more power, but if space is limited and you want the most mining power per device, this is it.
