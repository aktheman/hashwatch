HashWatch is the ultimate monitoring companion for BitAxe Bitcoin miners. Track real-time hashrate, temperature, power consumption, and pool statistics across all your miners from one beautiful dashboard.

FEATURES:
• Real-time miner monitoring with live hashrate, temperature, and power data
• Fleet health scoring with predictive maintenance alerts
• Pool analytics with earnings tracking and efficiency comparison
• Custom dashboards with configurable widget layouts
• Multi-miner support with group organization and auto-assignment
• Dark pool network stats — contribute anonymously to collective intelligence
• Firmware OTA updates — flash BitAxe firmware directly from the app
• Team collaboration — share miners with team members via email
• Push notifications for offline alerts, temperature warnings, and more
• Beautiful dark theme with 14 built-in themes and custom theme editor
• CSV/JSON data export and PDF report generation
• Web push notifications and PWA support
• Activity feed tracking all miner events
• World map view with geographic miner locations
• AI-powered anomaly detection and uptime forecasting

SUPPORTS:
• All BitAxe hardware models (Gamma, Ultra, Max, etc.)
• Braiins, Luxor, and custom mining pools
• iOS 16.4+ and Android 14+
• Web browsers (PWA)

PRICING:
• Free: Monitor up to 5 miners with basic features
• Pro: Unlimited miners, firmware updates, dark pool, AI anomaly detection, priority support

HashWatch does not mine cryptocurrency. It is a monitoring and management tool for existing BitAxe mining hardware.
